## Slide 2: Problem Statement
* Lack of continuous health monitoring in rural/urban communities.
* Manual and fragmented health records leading to delayed diagnosis.
* High hospital burden due to lack of early detection for chronic conditions.
* Difficulty in identifying community-wide disease outbreaks in real-time.

## Slide 3: Proposed Solution
* A centralized platform for real-time health data collection and monitoring.
* **Predictive Analytics:** Using AI/ML to identify high-risk patients before emergencies occur.
* **Unified Records:** Digital health IDs for every community member.
* **Emergency Response:** Automated alerts to doctors and family members with GPS tracking.

## Slide 4: System Architecture
* **Frontend:** Web Dashboard (HTML/CSS/JS) & Mobile App.
* **Backend:** Node.js & Express.js API.
* **Database:** MongoDB for scalable health records.
* **IoT Layer:** Integration with sensors (Heart Rate, SpO2, Temp) via APIs.
* **AI Layer:** Python-based ML models for risk prediction.

## Slide 5: Key Features
* 👤 **Patient & Doctor Portals:** Role-based access.
* ❤️ **Real-time Vitals:** Live tracking of health metrics.
* 🚨 **Smart Alerts:** Instant SMS/Email/Push notifications for abnormal vitals.
* 🤖 **AI Risk Score:** Predictive health assessment.
* 📊 **Community Dashboard:** Geographic heatmaps of health trends.

## Slide 6: Technology Stack
* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (React optional).
* **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js.
* **Database:** MongoDB (Atlas).
* **AI/ML:** Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Python.
* **DevOps/Cloud:** AWS/Google Cloud, Firebase for Auth.

## Slide 7: Use Case Diagram
* **Patient:** View vitals, book appointments, receive alerts.
* **Doctor:** Monitor assigned patients, provide consultations.
* **Health Worker:** Field data entry, manage community health.
* **Admin:** View analytics, manage system users.

## Slide 8: Innovation & Uniqueness
* **Early Outbreak Detection:** Identifies trends (e.g., rising fever cases in a specific street) before it becomes an epidemic.
* **Offline Capability:** Designed for low-connectivity rural areas (Sync when online).
* **Multi-lingual Support:** Accessible to non-English speakers (Tamil/Hindi etc.).

## Slide 9: Impact & Benefits
* **Faster Response:** Reduces emergency response time by 40%.
* **Cost Effective:** Lowers long-term healthcare costs through preventative care.
* **Empowerment:** Gives citizens control over their own health data.
* **Data-Driven Policy:** Helps government allocate resources based on real-time data.

## Slide 10: Future Scope
* **Smartwatch Integration:** Direct data sync from Apple Health/Google Fit.
* **Blockchain Security:** Ensuring 100% privacy and non-tamperable health records.
* **Voice Assistant:** "Ask my health" via Alexa/Google Assistant in local languages.
