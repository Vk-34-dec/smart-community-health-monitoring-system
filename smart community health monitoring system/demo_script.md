# Project Demo Script

**Total Time:** 5-7 Minutes

---

## 1. Introduction (30 Seconds)
* **Presenter:** "Good morning judges. We are Team [Team Name], and today we present our **Smart Community Health Monitoring System**."
* **Hook:** "In many rural areas, the nearest hospital is hours away. Our platform brings the hospital's monitoring capabilities to the community's doorstep."

## 2. Dashboard Overview (1 Minute)
* **Action:** Show the Home/Dashboard page (`index.html`).
* **Script:** "Here is our main dashboard. As an Admin, I can see the total number of patients, active appointments, and real-time emergency alerts. The charts here show the overall health trends of the community."

## 3. Real-time Monitoring & Alerts (1.5 Minutes)
* **Action:** Navigate to 'Emergency Alerts' or show the sidebar vitals.
* **Script:** "Our system integrates with IoT sensors. If a patient's heart rate or SpO2 levels cross a dangerous threshold, the system immediately triggers an alert. You can see the 'Recent Alerts' panel here showing a High BP alert for Ram Kumar 2 minutes ago."
* **Highlight:** "This isn't just a list; an automated SMS is already sent to his assigned doctor and family member."

## 4. AI Risk Prediction (1 Minute)
* **Action:** Navigate to 'AI Prediction'.
* **Script:** "Beyond just monitoring, we use AI to predict risks. Based on historical data and current vitals, our ML model identifies patients who are at high risk of chronic conditions like Diabetes or Hypertension before symptoms even become severe."

## 5. Patient Records & Appointments (1 Minute)
* **Action:** Navigate to 'Health Records' or 'Patients'.
* **Script:** "Every community member has a digital health ID. Doctors can instantly access their history, medications, and previous test results, reducing the need for physical paperwork and preventing errors."

## 6. Conclusion & Impact (1 Minute)
* **Script:** "Our solution is scalable, multi-lingual, and designed for impact. We aim to reduce the emergency response time by 40% and help government bodies make data-driven healthcare decisions."
* **Closing:** "Thank you for your time. We are now open for questions."

---

## Potential Q&A Tips
* **Data Privacy?** "We use AES-256 encryption for health records and comply with standard health data privacy norms."
* **Low Connectivity?** "The system is designed with a 'Local First' approach using Service Workers to cache data and sync when connectivity returns."
* **Cost?** "The software is open-source based, and the hardware uses affordable IoT sensors making it highly cost-effective for rural implementation."
